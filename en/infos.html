FREQUENT QUESTIONS
Do I need a precise idea?

No. An experience, sensation, a few references or an intention can be enough to start the discussion.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. The project is shown before the tattoo appointment and can evolve from your feedback, including significantly.

Do you reproduce Pinterest tattoos?

No. References can be material, but I do not reproduce existing tattoos. The goal is to build a piece that belongs to you.

Does carte blanche mean I have no control?

No. Carte blanche means freedom of interpretation, not absence of dialogue. You bring the intention; I take responsibility for its graphic translation.
